# appendix c

This appendix collects the drawing prompts for all 32 figures. Each prompt has two parts, "Drawing style" and "Drawing content", and can be copied on its own into a drawing tool; the style requirements are consistent with [A.7 Figure Style and Drawing Prompt Conventions](/broken/pages/daca76f0ae5fb9896fe9912791897d58aa86ad0f#a-7), and nodes, directions and conditions follow the source of the corresponding figure.

## Figure 01: Relationship Between MEMO Agent State Layer Products and Infrastructure

Corresponding figure: [Figure 01](file:///5907924/README.md#figure-01) (Overall product relationships)

```
Drawing style:
Draw a 16:9 landscape, research-paper-style figure. Use only a pure white background, black text, black outlines and black connectors; color, gray fills, gradients, shadows, 3D and decorative illustration are prohibited. Use regular rectangles, a uniform line weight, a regular grid and ample whitespace, and keep the inside of every node pure white. Distinguish relationships with solid lines, dashed lines, numbers and text labels, and do not encode meaning with color or line-weight differences. Text must be clearly legible with a uniform font size within the same level, and product names, protocol names and field names must be spelled exactly; avoid text overlapping connectors. Place at most 5 main nodes in a row, and split complex flows into layers or zones according to their content so they fit the landscape canvas. Output a black-and-white 4K PNG (3840 × 2160) or an editable SVG, and keep it clear when reduced and when printed in black and white.

Drawing content:
Use a top-to-bottom zoned architecture layout with at most four main rectangles per layer, and route cross-zone connectors along the outside. At the top, place "User / Organization", "Data Wallet: user control entry" and "Agent / Application / Framework"; the user points to Data Wallet, and Data Wallet and Agent / Application / Framework each point to "MEMO Agent Gateway: identity, delegation, policy, receipts". Gateway points to "MEMO Vault: state objects, versions, encryption, lifecycle"; Vault points separately to "Local / Hosted" and to "MEFS: long-term storage, redundancy, repair, storage proofs". Place "MEMO Proof: provenance, runtime evidence, Audit Root, storage proofs" in the lower part; Gateway, Vault and MEFS each feed into Proof with their own arrow, and Proof then points to "On-chain commitments / verification contracts / challenge and adjudication".
Keep "MEMO Protocol" at the side, connected to Gateway, Vault and Proof by three solid lines without arrowheads to express the foundation relationship. Connect "Meeda / FileProof / Attestation" to Proof with a solid arrow; connect "Compute Platform / TEE / x402 / Data Market" to Gateway with dashed arrows labeled "optional extension". The legend distinguishes directed relationships, undirected foundation relationships, and extensions selected by the deployment configuration. Keep Local / Hosted as a single node and do not draw a connector from it to Proof. The on-chain zone receives only the evidence and registrations shown; do not draw state plaintext or keys flowing on-chain; components connect through versioned interfaces and the registry.
```
